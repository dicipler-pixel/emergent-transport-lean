# Provenance

`EmergentTransport/Basic.lean` and the three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this
repository from *Directional Transport Geometry of Admissible Channels: Subspace Persistence,
Refraction, and Directional Support Sets* (29 June 2026). That is the edition in Jeromie Beasley's
Drive closest to the Zenodo record DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21089303, whose record titles read
*Intrinsic Emergent Transport Geometry* and *Integrated Quadralogy Architecture: Directional
Transport Geometry*. The results are Propositions 2 and 3, Lemma 6, Theorems 7, 13 and 14.
